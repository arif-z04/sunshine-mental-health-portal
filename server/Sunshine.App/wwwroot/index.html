Frequently Asked Questions

Got Questions? We Have Answers

How do virtual sessions work? ▾

Once you book an appointment, a secure session schedule is reserved. If advance payment is required, completing the payment confirms the booking. On the appointment day, you can connect directly with your clinician.

Which payment channels are supported in Bangladesh? ▾

We support bKash, Nagad, Rocket, and all major Bangladeshi Visa and MasterCard debit/credit cards in Bangladeshi Taka (৳ BDT).

Is my counseling session confidential? ▾

Yes, 100%. Only you and your certified practitioner have access to your consultation history and clinical notes. Sunshine adheres strictly to healthcare data privacy and minimization principles.

What happens if I need emergency assistance? ▾

Sunshine is an outpatient telecounseling platform, not an emergency crisis intervention room. In urgent crisis or suicidal ideation, please immediately call কান পেতে রই (+8801779554391), 999, or 16263.
