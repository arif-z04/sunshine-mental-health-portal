# What is Software?

## 1. What is it?
**Software** is a collection of instructions, data, and programs that tell physical computer hardware how to perform tasks.

## 2. Why does it exist?
Computer chips (silicon processors) are capable of billions of electrical operations every second, but they have no imagination or independent will. They cannot decide to display a calendar, calculate consultation fees in Bangladeshi Taka, or schedule an appointment. Software exists to guide that raw computing power into useful human solutions.

## 3. Real-World Analogy
Think of an orchestra.
* The physical instruments (violins, flutes, drums) are the **hardware**.
* The sheet music detailing notes, rhythm, and tempo is the **software**.
* The musician reading the sheet music is the **processor (CPU)**.

## 4. How it is used in Sunshine
Sunshine is full-stack healthcare software consisting of:
1. **Frontend software**: Instructs the browser to render buttons, doctor cards, and bKash payment modals.
2. **Backend software**: Calculates available evening slots, hashes passwords with PBKDF2, and checks permissions.
3. **Database software**: Persists medical records, appointment ledgers, and transactions.
