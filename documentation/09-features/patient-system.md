# Patient Care Portal System

The Patient Care Portal ([`/patient`](file:///home/noir/Desktop/Another-Sunshine-Project/server/Sunshine.App/wwwroot/patient/index.html)) provides mental health clients with a complete self-service toolkit.

---

## 1. Core Modules
1. **Specialist Finder**: Filter Bangladesh psychiatrists and clinical psychologists by specialty.
2. **Dynamic Slot Picker**: Select available evening consultation slots (16:00 to 20:30 BST).
3. **MFS Payment Gateway**: Pay for sessions in ৳ BDT via bKash or Nagad.
4. **My Appointments**: Real-time list of upcoming sessions with one-click Reschedule and Cancel buttons.
5. **My Profile**: Update personal phone number, date of birth, blood group, and emergency contact details.
