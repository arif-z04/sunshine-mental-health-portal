# Doctor Practice Portal System

The Doctor Practice Portal ([`/doctor`](file:///home/noir/Desktop/Another-Sunshine-Project/server/Sunshine.App/wwwroot/doctor/index.html)) empowers clinicians to manage their tele-psychiatry practice.

---

## 1. Clinical Features
* **Verification Status**: Visual badge indicating whether the doctor's BMDC registration has been verified by the Sunshine Medical Board.
* **Consultation Management**: View upcoming appointments, patient names, and session times.
* **Progress Notes**: Write and save confidential clinical consultation notes.
* **Policy Configuration**: Toggle payment policy between:
  - `ADVANCE`: Patient must pay within 15 minutes to secure booking.
  - `POST_PAYMENT`: Patient can pay during or after the consultation.
