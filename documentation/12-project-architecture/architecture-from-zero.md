# Software Architecture from Scratch

## 1. What is Software Architecture?
Software architecture is the fundamental organization of a system—its components, their relationships to each other, and the principles guiding its design.

## 2. Real-World Analogy: Building a Hospital
You do not build a hospital with emergency surgery, patient rooms, the cafeteria, and parking lots all dumped into one giant open warehouse. You design distinct wings with clear pathways between them.
