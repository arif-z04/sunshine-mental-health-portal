# Healthcare Confidentiality & Patient Privacy

Mental health data is uniquely sensitive and requires exceptional discretion.

---

## 1. Privacy Principles in Sunshine
* **Display Name Autonomy**: Patients can register and consult using a preferred first name or pseudonym.
* **Private Clinical Notes**: Consultation notes written by a doctor are visible **only to that doctor** and cannot be queried by unauthorized parties.
* **Non-Emergency Disclaimer**: Every portal clearly states that Sunshine is an outpatient tele-counseling service and provides immediate hotlines (**কান পেতে রই**, **999**) for acute psychiatric emergencies.
