# PostgreSQL 18 from Scratch

## 1. What is PostgreSQL?
**PostgreSQL** (often called *Postgres*) is the world's most advanced, open-source object-relational database system. It has been actively developed for over 35 years.

## 2. Why We Use PostgreSQL in Sunshine
1. **Rock-Solid Reliability**: Full ACID compliance (Atomicity, Consistency, Isolation, Durability) guarantees your medical data is never lost.
2. **Advanced Concurrency**: Partial unique indexes allow us to prevent double-booking at the database engine level.
3. **UTF-8 Native Support**: Stores Bengali script (বাংলা) and English seamlessly.
